## Key Features Implemented

1. **"Today's Word" Daily Hero:** Daily scripture, sermon tape excerpt, and instant 1-tap playback.
2. **Persistent Bottom Audio/Tape Player:** Stays active across all page navigations with speed control (1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2.0x) and low-data optimization.
3. **"Journey of the Week" Visual Timeline:** Sunday service -> Wednesday Bible study -> Friday Prayer -> Saturday Youth/Instrumental rehearsal.
4. **Quick-Action Strip:** Direct access to Consultation, Pastoral Visit, Baptism, Suggestion Box, Enquiry, and Give (sticky on mobile).
5. **Care & Pastoral Ministries Suite:**
   - Wellbeing Check-ups
   - First-time Visitor & Convert Follow-up
   - Pastoral Home/Hospital Visits (Confidential)
   - Add Enquiry with unique `CR-XXXX` tracking tickets
   - Suggestion Box (Anonymous or named, never public)
   - Spiritual Consultation booking with Pastor Oke Hyacinth Ayogu
6. **Baptism Suite & Certificate Generator:**
   - Scriptural foundations according to Acts 2:38
   - Candidate request form with under-18 parental consent
   - Preparation class roster & candidate tracker
   - Automated printable/downloadable Baptism Certificate with church seal
7. **"Only Believe" Hymns Space:**
   - Public domain & church-recorded instrumental catalog
   - Search by hymn number, title, or first line
   - Hymn of the Week & member Sunday hymn request form
   - Anointed instrumental music only (no choir)
8. **Youth Alive Sub-Brand:**
   - Dynamic amber/jade visual design
   - Still Waters retreat previews, youth rallies, and mentorship
9. **Online Giving & Bank Details:**
   - Paystack/Flutterwave simulated checkout
   - Ecobank Nigeria copyable account details for Naira & USD diaspora giving
10. **Interactive Enugu Location & Map:**
    - Pinned to Independence Layout beside IMT Campus 2
    - "Plan My Visit" first-timer guide with WhatsApp alert
11. **Staff CMS & Care Inbox:**
    - Role-based views: Pastor, Admin, Care Team, Media Team, Music Lead, Youth Leader
    - Unified care triage inbox with status updates and private pastoral notes
    - Baptism register and certificate issuance
    - Sermon upload and members-only gating controls
